## Mission

Papert Lab investigates how students solve complex problems and how intelligent systems can scaffold deeper thinking. We build pedagogical systems grounded in cognitive science and evaluate them through controlled experiments.

The lab is named for [Seymour Papert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seymour_Papert) (1928–2016), the mathematician, computer scientist, and educator whose constructionist theory of learning — the idea that people learn most effectively by constructing artifacts in the world — remains the intellectual foundation of our approach.

---

## Principles

**01 — Construction over consumption**
People learn by making things — programs, arguments, experiments. Passive exposure to information is a weak pedagogical instrument. We build systems that force students to specify, test, and revise.

**02 — Theories before systems**
Every pedagogical system encodes a theory of learning. We make those theories explicit, ground them in cognitive science, and test the predictions they make — not just the learning gains.

**03 — Requirement engineering as the core skill**
The most important skill in AI-assisted programming is not prompting — it is specifying. Students who can articulate precise requirements produce better code, better prompts, and better systems. This is what we teach.

**04 — Honest evaluation**
We run randomized controlled trials. We report null results. We distinguish learning gains from engagement metrics. Rigor in evaluation is not a constraint — it is the point.
