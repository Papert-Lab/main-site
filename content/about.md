Papert Lab is an applied cognitive science research lab that models problem solving and learning and designs systems to understand and augment memory, learning, and curiosity. We design intelligent tutoring systems and explore new interaction paradigms between AI agents and humans.

Our current research uses existing theories of scientific discovery and productive struggle to characterize how novices design specifications when interacting with agentic coding systems. We submit our work to HCI and computing education venues, run qualitative think-aloud studies, pilot new pedagogical paradigms in undergraduate courses, and host weekly discussion forums on augmenting cognition with computers.

Papert Lab was founded in 2025 by [Hudson Mitchell-Pullman](https://hudsonmp.github.io), an independent researcher and high school student. Hudson collaborates with and is mentored by [David H. Smith](https://hamiltonfour.tech/), [Annapurna Vadaparty](https://apvadaparty.github.io/), [Qianou Ma](https://qianouma.com/), among others.
