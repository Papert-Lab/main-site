Papert Lab studies how students develop reasoning, computational thinking, and self-directed expertise alongside AI systems.

---
- [Requirement Engineering Through Software Testing](/research/requirement-engineering/) | Using software testing as a pedagogical vehicle to teach requirements engineering. AI-assisted programming requires students to specify ill-defined system behavior; testing is the discipline that builds this skill.
- [Context Engineering for AI-Assisted Programming](/research/context-engineering/) | How learners construct the context that elicits high-quality reasoning from LLM coding assistants. Recasts context engineering as a continuation of requirements engineering rather than a separate practice.
