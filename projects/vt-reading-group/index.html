Virginia Tech Research Reading Group

Papert Lab is collaborating with the ASCEND³ Lab on computer science education research. We will be hosting a weekly reading group from May through September.

The purpose of the reading group is to foster a milieu of academics, researchers, students, and practitioners at Virginia Tech. Instead of focusing on specific research topics, we will explore research more broadly through reading seminal works on fostering an intellectually curious research community.

The reading group is open to all Virginia Tech faculty, undergraduate and graduate students, and the broader Blacksburg community who are curious and enjoy collaborating with researchers across domains.
