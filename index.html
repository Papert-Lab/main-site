Research

Papert Lab investigates how students solve complex problems and how intelligent systems can scaffold deeper thinking. Our work spans requirement engineering pedagogy, self-directed learning, and context engineering for large language models.

Named for Seymour Papert (1928–2016), whose constructionist theory of learning remains foundational to our approach.
